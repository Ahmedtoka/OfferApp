# Market price check, 2026-10-31 (internal, not for the listing)

Purpose: the owner approved $19.99 per month before a market check (spec §9). This reports what was found. **The price in the code, site and listing draft is unchanged.**

Method: one web search for current prices of buy-one-get-one apps on the Shopify App Store. Figures come from search results (App Store listing snippets and third-party blogs), were not opened and confirmed on each listing, and several sources disagree. Treat them as indicative and re-check each listing before deciding.

Reading: most simple BOGO apps start between about $1 and $15 per month and many tier by Shopify plan; $19.99 flat sits at the upper end for the simple tier and well below the larger suites. Pairly's flat price for every plan is a difference from the tiered apps, and unlimited orders is its stated benefit. This is a market observation, not advice: whether to keep, lower, or tier the price is the owner's decision.
